To assist you better in finding your dream home or commercial space, we collect information regarding your specific requirements, such as:

  • Preferred property type (Residential Flats, Villas, Commercial Shops, Plots).
  • Budget range, preferred locations, and expected timeline for possession.

When you visit our website, our servers automatically record certain technical information to enhance user experience and website security. This data does not personally identify you and includes:

Laxminarayan Developers operates on the principle of data minimization, meaning we only use your data for specific, legitimate business purposes. We may use your information to:

  • Service Delivery: To respond promptly to your property inquiries, provide detailed project brochures, floor plans, and pricing information.
  • Site Visits and Meetings: To schedule, confirm, and manage property site visits with our sales executives.
  • Transaction Management: To process property bookings, draft legal agreements, and manage the administrative aspects of your property purchase.
  • Customer Support: To provide post-sales support, handle grievances, and keep you updated on the construction progress of your chosen property.
  • Marketing and Communications: To send you personalized offers, newsletters, updates on new project launches, and event invitations. (You have the right to opt-out of marketing communications at any time).

  • Legal Compliance: To fulfill our legal and regulatory obligations as a real estate developer under local and national laws, including RERA (Real Estate Regulatory Authority) reporting and anti-money laundering checks.

3. How We Protect and Secure Your Data

We understand that the security of your personal and financial information is of paramount importance. Laxminarayan Developers has implemented robust physical, electronic, and managerial procedures to safeguard your data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

  • Data Encryption: All sensitive information transmitted through our website is encrypted using Secure Socket Layer (SSL) technology.
  • Access Control: Access to your personal data is strictly limited to authorized employees, sales representatives, and trusted service providers who need the information to perform their specific job duties. All personnel are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Regular Audits: We routinely review our data collection, storage, and processing practices to ensure compliance with modern security standards.

Disclaimer: While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

Laxminarayan Developers strictly adheres to a "No-Sell" policy. We do not sell, rent, trade, or lease your personal information to third-party marketing companies or data brokers under any circumstances.

We may share your information only in the following limited situations:

  • Trusted Third-Party Service Providers: We may engage third-party companies to assist us in our operations, such as CRM software providers, email marketing platforms, IT support, and customer service centers. These partners only have access to the data necessary to perform their functions and are legally obligated to keep it confidential.

  • Legal and Regulatory Authorities: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or government request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Laxminarayan Developers, our customers, or the public. This includes sharing information with RERA authorities as mandated.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website utilizes "Cookies" to provide a seamless and personalized browsing experience. A cookie is a small text file placed on your device's hard drive by a web server.

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the basic functioning of the website (e.g., remembering your session).
  • Analytical Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our site (e.g., Google Analytics) so we can improve content.
  • Advertising Cookies: May be used to deliver relevant advertisements to you on third-party platforms based on your browsing history.

You can choose to accept or decline cookies. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can usually modify your browser settings to decline cookies if you prefer. However, this may prevent you from taking full advantage of the website.

We believe you should have control over your personal data. Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights regarding your information:
