Frequently Asked Questions

Are there any extra/hidden charges apart from the property cost?

Expenses such as stamp duty, document registration charges, GST, GEB electric meter charges, and loan document fees are not included in the property price and are to be paid separately by the buyer.

Can I make modifications to the exterior or interior of my shop?

To maintain the uniform look of the building, changes to the exterior elevation are strictly not allowed. However, you can make internal modifications, for which the extra work charges must be paid in advance.

Is bank loan facility available for purchasing a shop/office?

Yes, you can apply for a commercial property loan. We will provide the necessary legal documents required by banks or financial institutions to process your loan.

What kind of businesses are ideal for Apple Square?

Located near major companies like L&T and Reliance , Apple Square is an excellent spot for Hospitals, Banks, ATMs, Medical Stores, Restaurants, Coffee Shops, Jewelry Showrooms, and Educational Classes.

Are there common washroom facilities available?

Yes, for the convenience of business owners and visitors, we have provided well-maintained common toilet facilities with proper water-proofing.

What happens if the developer changes the building plan later?

The developers reserve the absolute right to make necessary changes to the layout plan, internal plan, and elevation as per situational requirements.

Is this brochure a legal document?

No, the plans and details shown in the brochure are for representational purposes and easy understanding; they do not constitute a legal document.
