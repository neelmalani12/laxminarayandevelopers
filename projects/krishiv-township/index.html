About Krishiv Township

Step into a world where your dream home becomes a reality with Krishiv Township, a serene residential development by Laxminarayan Developers. Featuring premium residential plots and meticulously designed villas, this project is crafted to offer a peaceful and harmonious lifestyle. It is built to accommodate the needs of every family member, offering lush green gardens, a dedicated children's play area, and relaxing spaces like a gazebo for senior citizens. Situated in the tranquil surroundings of Pinjarat Dabhari Road, near Sojitra Farm, Surat, it offers a perfect escape from the city's hustle while staying beautifully connected to nature. It is the perfect destination for families seeking a safe, green, and luxurious environment to build their complete life.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the available plot and unit sizes?

The township primarily offers two plot sizes: 16'x 65' and 20'x 65', with detailed ground and first-floor unit plans available for each .

What kind of amenities are provided in the township?

Residents have access to a variety of facilities including a landscaped garden area, children's play area, gazebo, street lights, and a secure environment with a main entry gate and security cabin .

How wide are the internal roads in the project?

The township is designed with spacious internal RCC roads, featuring widths of 12.00 meters and 7.50 meters for easy connectivity.

What are the construction specifications for the homes?

Each home features 2'0" x 4'0" porcelain tiles, a black granite kitchen platform with an SS sink, modular switches, and a 1500-liter overhead water tank.

Are there any additional charges apart from the property price?

No, changes to the external elevation are not permitted to maintain the aesthetic uniformity of the township. However, internal changes can be made with extra charges.

Is this brochure a legal document?

No, the plans and details shown in the brochure are for representational purposes and easy understanding; they do not constitute a legal document.
