About Narmade Weekend Home (Phase-2)

Step into a "green way of living" with Narmade Weekend Home, a premier residential farm house development by Akshar Developers. Designed with the philosophy of "Caring Nature for Caring Life," this project offers a grand environment where luxury and nature coexist harmoniously. Perfectly situated at Moje Zanor on Shukaltirth Road, Bharuch, near the serene Narmada River, it provides the ideal balance between a peaceful sanctuary and modern accessibility. Each farm house is thoughtfully crafted to include a private swimming pool, personal garden, and gazebo, making it the perfect destination for families seeking a safe, well-structured, and magnificent place to enjoy a luxury lifestyle.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the available plot sizes for the farmhouses?

The project offers spacious plots, with built-up areas ranging from approximately 3141 Sq. Ft. to 4948 Sq. Ft.

What private amenities are included with each farmhouse?

Every farmhouse is designed for luxury, featuring a private swimming pool, a personal garden, a dedicated gazebo, air-conditioned bedrooms, and a personal water borewell for a 24/7 water supply.

What common amenities and security features does the project offer?

The entire project is secured with a compound wall and an attractive entrance gate featuring a security cabin. Residents can also enjoy well-planned RCC roads with paver blocks, street lights, a lush green common garden, and a children's play area.

What are the construction specifications for the homes?

The homes are constructed with a strong RCC frame structure. Interiors feature premium 24" x 24" Granamite (vitrified) flooring, granite kitchen platforms with stainless steel sinks, decorative main doors, and concealed wiring by standard companies.

Am I allowed to modify the exterior elevation of my farmhouse?

No, to maintain the aesthetic uniformity and beauty of the entire project, any alterations to the external elevation are strictly not permitted.

Are there any additional charges apart from the property price?

Yes, the charges for the GEB electric meter connection are to be paid separately. Additionally, any applicable government taxes, including GST and other local taxes, must be borne by the property buyer.

Is this brochure a legal document?

No, the plans and details shown in the brochure are for representational purposes and easy understanding; they do not constitute a legal document.
