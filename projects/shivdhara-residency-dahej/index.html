About Shivdhara Residency (Dahej)

Step into a life of peace and serenity at Shivdhara Residency, a prestigious development by Laxminarayan. Located in the fast-growing and tranquil area of Dahej Gam in Bharuch, this premium residential project offers the perfect foundation for your dream home. Featuring elegant row houses and variable plot sizes, it comes with essential amenities like an attractive main entry gate, a secure gated compound, and reliable water storage facilities. With N.A. Plan passed and loan facilities available, Shivdhara Residency is designed to provide a comfortable, secure, and hassle-free lifestyle for your family, perfectly connected to major industrial hubs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a bank loan available for this project?

Yes, this is a fully N.A. Plan Passed project , making it easy to avail of loan facilities for purchasing a house or plot.

What are the kitchen specifications?

The kitchen will feature a black granite platform with a stainless steel (SS) sink. Additionally, glazed dedo tiles will be provided up to a height of 6'0" above the platform.

What additional costs will I have to pay apart from the property price?

Besides the property cost, buyers will need to pay separately for the G.E.B. electric meter connection charges , applicable government taxes (like GST) , and monthly maintenance charges.

What type of electrical fittings will be used?

The house will be equipped with modern modular switches. Separate points for a T.V. and cable will also be provided in the hall.

What is the height of the tiles in the bathroom and toilet?

Attractive dedo tiles will be installed up to a height of 6'0" in the toilets and bathrooms.

Is this brochure a legal document?

No, the plans and details shown in the brochure are for representational purposes and easy understanding; they do not constitute a legal document.
