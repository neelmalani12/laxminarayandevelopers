Frequently Asked Questions

What are the water and road facilities?

Water: There is a common underground tank for the entire campus, and a separate 500-liter water tank is provided for each block (house). Roads: Strong R.C.C. internal roads have been constructed within the campus.

What are the security arrangements?

The entire campus is secured with a 5'6" high compound wall. A magnificent and sturdy designer M.S. gate has been installed at the entrance.

Can I make changes to the exterior look (Elevation) of my house?

No. To maintain the uniformity of the project, no changes will be allowed in the exterior elevation or paint work. However, internal changes are permitted.

What are the conditions for internal changes or extra work?

If you wish to make any internal modifications or have additional work done, the charges for it will be extra. You must inform us in advance and make the payment before the work commences.

Will the electrical fittings be modern?

Yes, the entire house will feature concealed wiring with modular switches. Additionally, ready points for TV and cable connections will be provided in the hall.

Is this brochure a legal document?

No, the plans and details shown in the brochure are for representational purposes and easy understanding; they do not constitute a legal document.
